Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dental Masters of Alexandria ("we," "our," or "us") manages information through the Dental Masters Inventory mobile application ("the App").

1. Scope & Purpose

The App is a dedicated internal operational utility intended exclusively for authorized clinical personnel to manage inventory, catalog supplies, and record internal material intake and deductions.

2. Information Collection and Use

  • User Identifiers: The App processes employee identification identifiers (such as assigned numeric PINs and staff system IDs) strictly for authentication and associating stock checkout and intake actions with authorized personnel in internal audit logs.
  • No Protected Health Information (PHI): The App does not collect, record, process, or transmit patient medical records, treatment plans, or any Protected Health Information under HIPAA.

3. Device Permissions & Camera Access

  • Camera Usage: The App requires access to the device camera solely for scanning optical 1D/2D barcodes on clinical item packaging. The camera feed is processed locally on the device in real time and is never recorded, captured, or transmitted externally.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose user data or inventory records to commercial data brokers, advertising networks, or unauthorized third parties. Data is processed solely through our private cloud infrastructure (Supabase PostgreSQL).

5. Data Retention, Deletion, and Inquiries
